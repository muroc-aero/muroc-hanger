> **The W3C PROV / PROV-Agent meta-model.** The three PROV classes —
> *entity* (ellipse), *activity* (rectangle), *agent* (pentagon) — and the six
> core relations that connect them. Hangar's two provenance layers instantiate
> this base model: Layer 1 (the `@capture_tool` session graph) as a lighter,
> activity-centric call/decision trace, and Layer 2 (the `omd` artifact model)
> as full PROV extended with PROV-Agent's agent-centric associations for
> agentic AI workflows (arXiv:2508.02866). Class colours (yellow/blue/orange)
> follow the conventional PROV notation and are reused across all four figures.
