**Full caption:**

> **Layer 2 — the `omd` PROV-Agent artifact ontology.** The concrete PROV
> instance emitted by one `omd` plan run: versioned *entities* (yellow
> ellipses), *activities* (blue rectangles) and *agents* (orange pentagons)
> connected by typed `prov_edges` that record what was produced, from what,
> and how it verifies requirements. Solid edges are PROV relations — the six
> core relations plus the PROV-Agent domain relations (`verifies`,
> `satisfies`/`violates`, `has_criterion`, `partOf`, …); dashed edges are
> agent associations (`wasAssociatedWith`, `wasAttributedTo`). The full
> `entity_type` and `relation` vocabularies (`KNOWN_ENTITY_TYPES`,
> `KNOWN_PROV_RELATIONS`) are listed in the legend.
