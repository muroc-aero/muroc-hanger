# Task: Caravan Fuel Burn on a 250 NM Hop

Find the fuel a stock Cessna 208 Caravan burns on a simple three-phase
mission -- climb, cruise, descent, no takeoff roll -- using its standard
single-turboprop propulsion.

## The mission

- Range: 250 NM, cruising at 18,000 ft.
- Climb at 850 ft/min holding 104 knots equivalent airspeed.
- Cruise at 129 knots equivalent airspeed.
- Descend at 400 ft/min holding 100 knots equivalent airspeed.
- Integrate each phase on 11 points.

## What matters

- Use the stock Caravan definition that ships with the tooling rather than
  building the aircraft up from raw numbers.
- Confirm the mission solve converged, and judge whether the fuel burn is
  plausible for a Caravan over 250 NM; record that interpretation.

## Report

Fuel burn, operating empty weight, and maximum takeoff weight, all in kg.
